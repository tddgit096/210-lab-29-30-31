Project Overview:
    Simulates the relationship between a set of specific predatory animals and its population relative to a prey population and also disease vectors.
Implementation:
        3 maps containing 3 keys: Coyote, Lion, Orca. Each of these are signified by the Predator Class.
            within each map are lists signifying: 
                1. Members of the predatory population by name age health and hunger. Signified by Predator class
                2. Members of the prey population by name age and health. signified by Prey class
                3. Disease Vectors by name. Every time cycle: the prey will have a chance to be infected with it which might later infect predators that end up eating them.
Simulates:
    as prey population decreases over time due to predation, the predatory population will also decline with time as there is less prey for them to eat and they will starve.
    As predator population declines, this will encourage prey population to increase over time.
    prey and predators will both die of old age.
    Predators must each eat multiple prey. if a predator goes 2 years with insufficient food it dies.
    if there is a disease threat active a percentage of the prey population will contract disease's status at random. each year they have a culmulative chance of throwing it off, succumbing to it, or incubating it for another year.
    When a predator eats a creature with disease it will be affliced: 
    predator has a chance of either succumbing to an illness, dying from it, or throwing it off as per the prey rules. When diseased, predators have a less likely chance of successfully hunting.
